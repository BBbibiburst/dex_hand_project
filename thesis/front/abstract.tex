\begin{cabstract}
灵巧手操作需要通过多指接触完成抓取、搬移与装配，示教采集涉及反复操作、物体复位和设备维护，多指接触还增加了安全控制与操作协调难度。仿真便于控制初始条件、批量生成并重复执行任务，以及记录完整状态和接触信息，为扩大示教覆盖和分析接触过程提供了实验手段。然而，抓取方案的可执行性、示教数据的时序一致性以及仿真触觉与真实触觉的差异，直接影响这些数据的使用。本文围绕上述问题，研究灵巧手仿真示教数据构建与触觉跨域重建方法。

构建RealMAN（睿尔曼智能）RM75B机械臂与途见科技自研欠驱动灵巧手的多任务仿真平台，集成控制、任务执行与传感器记录。根据指节、指尖和手掌的几何差异构建曲面阵列，获得16个触觉区域、712维处理后观测，五类任务各一条历史记录的触觉数组均通过维度、有限性与非负性检查。在此基础上，将GraspQP官方评价指标适配到六驱动手部代理，参考DexEvolve设计实现进化搜索，并结合腕姿格点扩展、IK可达性、碰撞约束和连续轨迹检查，将抓取候选转化为专家示教，并对抬升轨迹进行严格回放验证。Sugar Box的10条历史局部扰动记录均通过严格回放验证，验证范围为同一物体及相近源抓取。

针对多模态示教的记录与使用，建立状态和动作的字段定义、执行前后观测的时序配对及数据转换流程，并结合来源、数值、连续性和重复关系开展分层质量检查。指定Sugar Box修复数据的340行导出动作、状态命令区及触觉与源记录对应字段的最大绝对差均为0；同时识别出历史命令不连续和状态字段缺失问题，明确不同版本数据的适用条件。

针对触觉跨域差异，利用真实数据的统计先验指导仿真物理探针构造伪配对，以独立双域变分自编码器学习时序表示，并训练门控循环单元网络映射两域潜变量。进一步引入共享参数向量场积分，探索对抗优化、多视角判别和残差修正，分别检查分布距离、邻域覆盖、输入依赖与判别器的跨会话表现。

实验表明，潜变量映射改进版本在原冻结验证协议的11个分类器实例中有2个联合通过。训练后的共享向量场单步基线在指定切片Wasserstein距离下，相对未转换仿真输入下降49.77\%。后续对抗优化的生成接受率可达100\%，但分布质量未同步改善，多视角残差终点出现明显距离退化。冻结审计进一步发现当前判别器未达到既定真实域接受要求。这些结果说明，接受率、分布相似性与输入对应关系需要分别验证。本文完成了仿真示教构建、指定数据质量验证及触觉跨域重建探索，完整触觉仿真到现实迁移与实机任务收益仍需进一步检验。
\end{cabstract}

\begin{eabstract}
Dexterous manipulation relies on multi-finger contact for grasping, transport, and assembly. Collecting demonstrations requires repeated operation, object resetting, and equipment maintenance, while multi-finger contact adds safety and coordination demands. Simulation enables controlled initial conditions, batch generation, repeatable execution, and access to complete states and contact information, supporting broader demonstration coverage and contact analysis. The usefulness of these data depends on executable grasps, consistent recording, and the gap between simulated and real tactile responses. This thesis investigates simulation-based demonstration data construction and cross-domain tactile reconstruction.

A multi-task platform integrates a RealMAN RM75B manipulator and an underactuated dexterous hand developed by Tachin-Tech, including control, task execution, and sensor recording. Curved arrays adapted to the finger segments, fingertips, and palm provide 16 sensing patches and 712 processed channels. One historical record from each of five tasks passes tactile dimension, finiteness, and non-negativity checks. Official GraspQP metrics are adapted to a six-drive hand surrogate, and evolutionary search is implemented with reference to DexEvolve. Wrist-pose lattice expansion, inverse-kinematics reachability, collision constraints, and continuous-trajectory checks connect grasp candidates to expert demonstrations, with strict replay verification for lifting trajectories. Ten historical Sugar Box records with local perturbations pass strict replay verification within the scope of one object and closely related source grasps.

Multimodal recording defines state and action fields, pre-action and post-action temporal pairing, and format conversion. Layered checks examine provenance, numerical validity, continuity, and duplication. For a specified repaired Sugar Box export, the maximum absolute differences for the action, command portion of the state, and tactile fields are all zero across 340 rows relative to their corresponding source fields. Historical command discontinuities and missing state fields are also identified, establishing version-specific conditions for data use.

Real tactile statistics guide simulated physical probes to construct pseudo-pairs. Independent variational autoencoders learn temporal representations for the two domains, connected by a gated recurrent unit mapping network. Shared-parameter vector-field integration, adversarial optimization, multi-view discrimination, and residual refinement are then explored. Evaluation separately examines distribution distances, neighborhood coverage, input dependence, and cross-session discriminator behavior.

The adapted latent mapping satisfies the joint requirements for two of eleven classifier instances under the original frozen validation protocol. The trained shared-field single-step baseline reduces the specified sliced Wasserstein distance by 49.77\% relative to unconverted simulation inputs. Subsequent adversarial optimization reaches 100\% generated-sample acceptance without corresponding distribution-quality improvement, while the multi-view residual endpoint exhibits substantial distance degradation. A frozen audit further finds that the current discriminator fails the prescribed real-domain acceptance requirement. Acceptance, distributional similarity, and input correspondence therefore require separate verification. This thesis establishes simulated demonstration construction, specified data-quality verification, and tactile reconstruction experiments; complete tactile sim-to-real transfer and real-robot task benefits require further evaluation.
\end{eabstract}
